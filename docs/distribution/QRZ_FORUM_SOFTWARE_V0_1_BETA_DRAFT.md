# QRZ forum draft - 6SNT.ADIF-HUB v0.1.0-beta

Hello,

I am Luis Soto, CA6SNT / 6SNT, from Valdivia, Chile.

I am preparing the first public beta of 6SNT.ADIF-HUB, a free local-first tool
for amateur radio operators who need to clean and consolidate log exports before
importing them into their main logbook or online services.

The app is not a logger. It is meant as a staging tool for copies of existing
logs. It can import formats such as ADIF/ADI, ADX, Cabrillo/CBR, CSV/TSV, XLSX
and JSON, normalize fields, detect duplicate QSO candidates, help review
conflicts and export a consolidated ADIF file.

Repository:
https://github.com/6SNT-RADIO/6SNT.ADIF-HUB

Release:
https://github.com/6SNT-RADIO/6SNT.ADIF-HUB/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta

Important: this is beta software. Please test with copies or small subsets of
your logs first, and review exported ADIF before uploading it to LoTW, eQSL,
ClubLog, QRZ or your main logger.

I would appreciate technical feedback from operators who have mixed exports,
contest logs, portable activation logs or old ADIF/Cabrillo files to clean up.
Reports with sanitized examples are welcome.
