# 60-day content calendar

## Week 1-2

- Prepare internal release.
- Review ES/EN docs.
- Create screenshots with sanitized data.
- Ask a discovery question in a trusted channel, without commercial link.

## Week 3-4

- Invite a small closed beta group.
- Share a short technical demo of ingest and mapper.
- Collect errors through the matrix.

## Week 5-6

- Publish a learning post about Cabrillo or Excel/CSV.
- Share beta notes without private data.
- Expand beta only if there are no blockers.

## Week 7-8

- Prepare release notes.
- Post a broader beta invitation in 1 or 2 communities.
- Answer questions with clear limitations.

## Rule

Do not post every day. Separate ES/EN messages and adapt tone to each community.
