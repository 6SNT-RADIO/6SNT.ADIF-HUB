# Target operator profiles

## Priority profiles

- Operators with large historical logs.
- Contesters exporting `Cabrillo`.
- Users of N1MM, Log4OM, DXKeeper, HAMRS or other loggers.
- POTA/SOTA activators.
- Operators keeping part of their log in Excel.
- Users concerned about privacy and local control.

## Selection criteria

Seek diversity of formats and workflows, not only expert users. The beta should include people who can explain why they would or would not trust the final export.

## Temporary exclusions

- Users needing direct cloud sync.
- Operators looking for a live logger.
- Flows that require CAT, PTT or transmission.
