# Product narrative

6SNT.ADIF-HUB is a local hub for operators who accumulate logs in several formats and need to clean them before uploading to their main platforms.

## Problem

QSOs get scattered across ADIF exports, Excel sheets, CSV files, Cabrillo contest logs and personal tools. The operator ends up with duplicates, incomplete fields and fear of polluting LoTW, eQSL, ClubLog or the primary logger.

## Promise

Clean and consolidate before uploading.

## What it is

- Local hub for ingest, normalization, deduplication, conflict resolution and ADIF export.
- Preparation and review tool.
- Intermediate layer between scattered files and external platforms.

## What it is not

- Not a live logger.
- Not a replacement for LoTW, eQSL, ClubLog, QRZ, Log4OM, N1MM or DXKeeper.
- Does not automate external uploads.
- Does not promise full compatibility with every historical format.

## Pillars

- Local-first.
- Privacy.
- Multiformat ingest.
- ADIF 3.1.7.
- Intelligent deduplication.
- Human control.
- Clean export.
